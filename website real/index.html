welcome to family dinner

family dinners started this year between me and my two friends, Lucia and Amelia, in an effort to increase the amount of casual time we spend together.

we realized that hanging out with our friends had become something that required either an abudnace of time, money, or elaborate plans, and with our schedules, that wasn't super feasible. so we began planning weekly dinners, every monday, rotating houses.

i chose family dinners for my website as a play on the suggestion of family recipes. the past three years, my friends have become my second family at washu. i also love cooking! so this website was a way for me to document my friendships, as well as provide a place to store recipes that i have made, or want to make.
